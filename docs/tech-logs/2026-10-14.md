## Engineering Retrospective & Key Architectural Lessons

### 1. The Edge Cases of Time in Cloud Deployments
- **Problem**: Cloud hosting platforms (Render, AWS, Fly.io) run on UTC by default. In West Africa (WAT / UTC+1), shops routinely trade past midnight. A standard midnight calendar rollover splits a single retail shift across two separate days.
- **Solution**: Shift business day calculations to 2:00 AM local time anchored strictly to `Africa/Lagos` via Go's `time.LoadLocation("Africa/Lagos")`.
- **Automation**: Store owners frequently fall asleep without closing out their ledger. A background worker (`backend/internal/ledger/worker.go`) wakes up at 2:02 AM WAT, auto-finalizes yesterday's open records, auto-fills null closings to `Opening + Receipts` (0 sales), updates master stock, and dispatches end-of-day SMS/email summaries.

### 2. Defense-in-Depth for Financial Privacy (Staff vs. Owner RBAC)
- **Problem**: In emerging market retail, store owners trust staff to count physical boxes on shelves, but strictly conceal product costs, profit margins, and daily revenue to protect their business and prevent staff theft/dispute.
- **Vulnerability**: Client-side UI column hiding is inadequate; tech-savvy staff can inspect API JSON payloads in browser DevTools.
- **Solution**: Enforce wire-level redaction on the backend. In `TodayHandler`, whenever `isStaff` is true, the server explicitly overwrites `entry.Price = 0` before serialization. Pricing data never traverses the network to staff sessions.

### 3. Navigating Telecom Regulations in Emerging Markets (Termii & NCC)
- **Problem**: Retailers in busy Nigerian open markets check SMS notifications far more reliably than email. However, under Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) anti-fraud guidelines, major carriers (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) prohibit custom alphanumeric Sender IDs (`Ifesquare`) without a verified Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) business incorporation certificate.
- **Solution**: Routed SMS notifications through Termii's pre-approved generic route (`talert`) on the `generic` channel. This eliminates regulatory review friction, requires zero CAC paperwork, and delivers transactional summaries immediately.

### 4. Dual-Stack DNS Traps ("Happy Eyeballs" in Node.js)
- **Problem**: Node.js 18+ defaults to resolving IPv6 and IPv4 concurrently. On local networks with IPv6 DNS responses but no outward IPv6 route, HTTPS requests to Cloudflare-proxied APIs (like Linear) hang for 10-30 seconds before timing out with `read ETIMEDOUT` / `fetch failed`.
- **Solution**: Prepend `dns.setDefaultResultOrder('ipv4first')` in Node CLI automation tools to force immediate IPv4 socket connections, dropping request latency from 30s timeouts down to under 300ms.
